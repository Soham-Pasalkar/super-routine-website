Why these go in the morning

Vitamins A, D, E and K are fat-soluble, so they are best taken with a meal. Breakfast is the most consistent meal for most people, which makes the routine easy to remember.

B vitamins, L-theanine and ginseng fit the start of the day, when energy metabolism and focus matter most. None of them is a stimulant and the Day Pack contains no caffeine.

Zinc is kept apart from the Night Pack's magnesium. At normal doses most people can take the two together, but minerals compete for absorption in the gut. Splitting them across morning and night removes the question. Read more in taking zinc and magnesium together.

Why 100% RDA and not megadoses

The vitamins and minerals are dosed to cover the daily requirement, not to reach the upper limits. That fills the gaps a typical diet leaves, while keeping you well clear of excess, even if you also eat fortified foods. If a doctor has told you that you are deficient in something specific, such as vitamin D or B12, follow the treatment dose they give you.

How to take it

  • Take one Day sachet after breakfast with water.
  • Take one Night sachet after dinner.
  • Do not take more than one Day sachet a day, and do not combine it with another multivitamin.
  • If you take warfarin, thyroid medicine, diabetes medicine, antibiotics or iron tablets, ask your doctor or pharmacist before use.

FAQ

Can I take the Day Pack on an empty stomach?

It is better after breakfast. Vitamins A, D, E and K absorb best with food that contains fat, and zinc can cause nausea in some people on an empty stomach.

Does the Day Pack contain caffeine?

No. There are no stimulants in the Day Pack. L-theanine and Panax ginseng are not stimulants.

Can I take the Day Pack with another multivitamin?

No. The Day Pack already gives 100% RDA of 13 vitamins and 7 trace minerals. Adding another multivitamin doubles your intake for no benefit.

Can I take the Day Pack and Night Pack together?

They are designed to be taken apart: Day after breakfast, Night after dinner. This keeps zinc and magnesium apart and puts ashwagandha in the evening wind-down.
