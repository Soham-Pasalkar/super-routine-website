Day Pack: your morning supplements in one sachet

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, FounderUpdated 22 Oct 2026

The Day Pack holds four nutrients that suit the morning: vitamin D3 + K2 (600 IU), vitamin B12 as methylcobalamin (1 mg), zinc picolinate (12 mg) and purified Himalayan shilajit (250 mg). Take one sachet after breakfast, ideally with a meal that contains some fat.

Why these four go in the morning

Vitamin D and K2 are fat-soluble, so they are best taken with a meal. Breakfast is the most consistent meal for most people, which makes it easy to remember.

Zinc is kept apart from the Night Pack's magnesium. At normal doses most people can take the two together, but very high zinc intakes can reduce magnesium absorption, and minerals compete for absorption in the gut. Splitting them across morning and night removes the question entirely. Read more in our zinc guide.

B12 and shilajit support the day's energy metabolism, so they sit with the morning routine. Neither is a stimulant and neither contains caffeine.

How to take it

  • Take one Day sachet after breakfast with water.
  • Take one Night sachet after dinner or 30–60 minutes before bed.
  • Do not take more than one Day sachet a day.
  • If you take iron tablets, antibiotics or thyroid medicine, ask your doctor or pharmacist about spacing them from the Day Pack.

FAQ

Can I take the Day Pack on an empty stomach?

It is better after breakfast. Vitamin D and K2 absorb best with food that contains fat, and zinc can cause nausea in some people on an empty stomach.

Does the Day Pack contain caffeine?

No. There are no stimulants in the Day Pack.

Can I take the Day Pack and Night Pack together?

They are designed to be taken apart: Day after breakfast, Night in the evening. This keeps zinc and magnesium apart and puts melatonin at bedtime, where it belongs.
