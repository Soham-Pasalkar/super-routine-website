How to take Super Routine daily supplements

  • Morning: one Day sachet after breakfast, with water. Vitamins A, D, E and K are fat-soluble, so a meal helps.
  • Evening: one Night sachet after dinner, with water. Omega-3 absorbs best with a meal.
  • Take both every day, at about the same times. Do not take more than one of each sachet a day.

Why Super Routine is split into day and night

Timing matters for some nutrients. Fat-soluble vitamins absorb best with breakfast, and L-theanine and ginseng suit the start of the day. Magnesium and ashwagandha fit an evening wind-down, and omega-3 goes with dinner. Zinc sits in the Day sachet and magnesium in the Night sachet so the two minerals are not taken together. Who should not take Super Routine

  • Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, or under 18.
  • People taking warfarin or other blood thinners: vitamin K2 can reduce warfarin's effect, and omega-3 and ginseng may affect clotting.
  • People taking thyroid medicine: iodine, selenium and ashwagandha can affect thyroid hormones.
  • People taking diabetes, blood pressure or sedative medicines, MAOI antidepressants or immunosuppressants: ginseng, chromium and ashwagandha may interact with them.
  • People with liver problems (rare liver injury has been reported with ashwagandha) or kidney disease (the risk of magnesium build-up is higher), and people with a fish allergy.
  • If you take antibiotics such as doxycycline or ciprofloxacin, take them at least 2 hours before, or 4–6 hours after, either sachet.

If any of these apply, or you take any regular medicine, ask your doctor before starting.
