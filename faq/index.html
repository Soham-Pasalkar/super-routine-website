How to take Super Routine daily supplements

  • Morning: one Day sachet after breakfast, with water. Vitamin D3 and K2 are fat-soluble, so a meal helps.
  • Evening: one Night sachet after dinner or 30–60 minutes before bed. It contains melatonin, so do not drive or operate machinery afterwards.
  • Take both every day, at about the same times. Do not take more than one of each sachet a day.

Why Super Routine is split into day and night

Timing matters for some nutrients. Melatonin signals night to the body clock, so it belongs in the evening, and magnesium fits a wind-down routine. Zinc sits in the Day sachet and magnesium in the Night sachet so the two minerals are not taken together. Who should not take Super Routine

  • Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, or under 18.
  • People with epilepsy, or taking blood thinners such as warfarin: melatonin needs medical supervision, and high doses of omega-3 may cause bleeding problems with anticoagulants.
  • People taking thyroid medicine, sedatives, diabetes or blood pressure medicines, or immunosuppressants: ashwagandha may interact with them.
  • People with liver problems (rare liver injury has been reported with ashwagandha) or kidney disease (the risk of magnesium build-up is higher).
  • If you take antibiotics such as doxycycline or ciprofloxacin, take them at least 2 hours before, or 4–6 hours after, the Night sachet.

If any of these apply, or you take any regular medicine, ask your doctor before starting.
