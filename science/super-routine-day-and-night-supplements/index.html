"Day and night supplements" simply means splitting a supplement routine by time of day, because a few nutrients work better, or are easier to take, at a particular time. Most of the evidence is about practical things: taking fat-soluble vitamins with a meal, keeping calming ingredients for the evening, and spacing nutrients that can compete. This page explains how the Super Routine day and night sachets apply those rules, and where timing matters less than marketing suggests.

Why the Day sachet goes after breakfast

Fat-soluble vitamins need a meal. According to the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, fat in the gut enhances vitamin D absorption, and vitamin K is absorbed along with dietary fat; the same is true of vitamins A and E. Breakfast is the most regular meal for most people, which makes the routine easy to keep. Our 600 IU of vitamin D3 matches the ICMR-NIN adult requirement and is well below the 4,000 IU upper limit.

Minerals are easier on a full stomach. Nausea is a known effect of zinc on an empty stomach. Our 17 mg of zinc is under the 40 mg upper limit, and copper is included to keep the two in balance.

L-theanine and ginseng suit the day. Both are studied for focus, mental performance and fatigue, and neither contains caffeine. Ginseng can disturb sleep in some people, which is another reason it is not in the Night sachet.

Why the Night sachet goes after dinner

Ashwagandha fits the evening. The NIH summarises a 2021 meta-analysis of five studies (372 adults) that found a small but significant benefit for sleep at 250–600 mg a day over 6–12 weeks, with larger effects at 600 mg. Drowsiness is one of its reported side effects. Our 600 mg matches the dose with the larger effects.

Magnesium and omega-3 are flexible. The NIH does not tie magnesium absorption to time of day, and omega-3 can be taken whenever suits you. We put them in the Night sachet because dinner is usually the largest meal, fish oil absorbs best with food, and it keeps magnesium away from the morning zinc.

The Night sachet contains no melatonin and no sedatives.

Why zinc and magnesium are in different sachets

To be accurate: at normal doses most people can take zinc and magnesium together. The interaction the NIH describes involves very high zinc intakes of about 142 mg a day, far above our 17 mg. We split them anyway because minerals are absorbed in the same part of the gut, and putting them in different sachets removes the question completely. Who should not take Super Routine day and night supplements

  • Anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, or under 18.
  • People taking warfarin or other blood thinners: vitamin K changes warfarin's effect, and omega-3 and ginseng may affect clotting.
  • People taking thyroid medicine or with a thyroid condition: iodine, selenium and ashwagandha can affect thyroid hormones.
  • People taking diabetes or blood pressure medicines, sedatives, MAOI antidepressants or immunosuppressants: ginseng, chromium and ashwagandha may interact with them.
  • People on statins (high vitamin D intakes may reduce the effect of some) or quinolone and tetracycline antibiotics (take them at least 2 hours before or 4–6 hours after either sachet).
  • People with kidney problems (magnesium build-up risk rises), liver problems (rare liver injury has been reported with ashwagandha), or a fish allergy.

Day and night sachets vs one multivitamin

The Day sachet already works as a complete multivitamin and mineral at 100% RDA, so do not add another one. What a once-a-day multivitamin cannot do is place calming ingredients in the evening or keep zinc and magnesium apart, and most do not contain omega-3, ashwagandha, L-theanine or ginseng. FAQ

What are day and night supplements?

They split a supplement routine by time of day: nutrients that absorb best with a meal or support the day go in the morning, and nutrients that fit an evening wind-down go at night.

Can I take the Super Routine day and night sachets at the same time?

No. Take the Day sachet after breakfast and the Night sachet after dinner. This keeps zinc and magnesium apart and puts ashwagandha in the evening, where its drowsiness is not a problem.

What if I forget the Day sachet?

Take it with your next meal the same day, not at bedtime, and never take two Day sachets to catch up.

Is Super Routine a multivitamin?

Partly. The Day sachet contains 13 vitamins and 7 trace minerals at 100% RDA, plus L-theanine and Panax ginseng. The Night sachet adds magnesium, ashwagandha and omega-3. It does not contain iron or calcium.
