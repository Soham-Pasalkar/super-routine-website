7 supplements you should not take together

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, Registered Pharmacist, FounderUpdated 21 Oct 2026

The main combinations to separate or avoid are calcium with iron, iron with zinc, high-dose zinc with copper or magnesium, melatonin with other sleep products, omega-3 or vitamin K with blood thinners, and two products with the same nutrient. Most can still be taken on the same day if you space them a few hours apart.

"Not together" rarely means "never". For most of the pairs below the problem is competition for absorption in the gut, which you solve by taking them at different times of day. A few are about safety, where the answer is to talk to your doctor first. Here is each combination, why it matters and what to do.

1. Calcium and iron

This is the pairing most people have heard of. The NIH Office of Dietary Supplements says calcium might interfere with iron absorption, although the effect has not been definitively established, and experts suggest taking individual calcium and iron supplements at different times of the day. A simple routine is iron in the morning and calcium with dinner, or the other way round.

Calcium absorbs best in small amounts: absorption from supplements is highest at doses of 500 mg or less. If you take more, split it across meals rather than taking it all at once.

2. Iron and zinc

Iron tablets of 25 mg of elemental iron or more, taken at the same time as zinc, can reduce zinc absorption. The iron added to fortified foods does not have a significant effect. If you have been prescribed iron, take it at a different time from any zinc supplement. With Super Routine, that means taking your iron away from the Day Pack, which contains 12 mg of zinc.

3. High-dose zinc and copper

Zinc and copper share an absorption pathway. Doses of 50 mg of zinc or more over a period of weeks can inhibit copper absorption and lead to low copper status. This is one reason the adult upper limit for zinc is 40 mg a day. Long courses of high-dose "immunity" zinc are the usual way people get there. Stay under 40 mg a day from all supplements combined unless a doctor has prescribed more.

4. Very high-dose zinc and magnesium

Very high zinc intakes from supplements, around 142 mg a day, might interfere with magnesium absorption. That is more than three times the zinc upper limit. At ordinary doses the two are commonly taken together. We cover this in detail in Can you take zinc and magnesium together? Super Routine still keeps them apart, with zinc in the morning and magnesium glycinate at night, so the question never comes up.

5. Melatonin and other sleep products

Melatonin appears in many sleep gummies, drinks and "night" blends, so it is easy to take two products that both contain it. Label accuracy is a real issue: NCCIH reports that in a 2023 study of melatonin gummies, the actual amount ranged from 74% to 347% of the labelled quantity. Stacking products multiplies that uncertainty.

Use one melatonin product only. The Super Routine Night Pack already contains 3 mg of melatonin, so do not add a separate melatonin supplement. NCCIH advises anyone taking medicine to speak to their health care provider before using melatonin, and says people with epilepsy and those on blood thinners need medical supervision. Ashwagandha might also interact with sedative medicines, so if you take a prescribed sleeping tablet or sedative, ask your doctor before adding either.

6. Omega-3 or vitamin K with blood thinners

This one is a supplement-and-medicine pairing, but it is the most important on the list.

  • Vitamin K and warfarin: people on warfarin need a consistent vitamin K intake from food and supplements, because sudden changes can increase or decrease the drug's effect. Do not start a product with vitamin K2, such as our vitamin D3 + K2, without your doctor's approval.
  • Omega-3 and anticoagulants: fish oil might prolong clotting time (raise INR). Most research found 3–6 g a day does not significantly affect anticoagulant status, but the NIH advises that people taking omega-3 with anticoagulants are monitored periodically. Tell your doctor before taking omega-3.

7. Two products that contain the same nutrient

The most common "combination" problem is not two different nutrients. It is the same nutrient from two products: a multivitamin, an immunity tablet and a separate zinc or vitamin D supplement. Vitamin D is the clearest example. The adult upper limit is 4,000 IU a day, and according to the NIH, vitamin D toxicity is almost always the result of excessive intake from supplements. If your doctor has prescribed high-dose vitamin D, follow that plan and tell them about any other vitamin D you take.

Minerals and common medicines

Minerals also bind to some medicines in the gut. These timings come from the NIH fact sheets:

  • Quinolone and tetracycline antibiotics (such as ciprofloxacin or doxycycline): take the antibiotic at least 2 hours before, or 4–6 hours after, zinc or magnesium. For calcium and quinolones, 2 hours before or after.
  • Levothyroxine (thyroid medicine): do not take it within 4 hours of calcium carbonate or iron supplements.
  • Bisphosphonates (such as alendronate): separate from magnesium by at least 2 hours.
  • Levodopa: iron supplements reduce its absorption.

FAQ

What vitamins should not be taken together?

The main ones to separate are calcium and iron, iron (25 mg or more) and zinc, and high-dose zinc with copper or magnesium. Also avoid taking two products that both contain vitamin D, zinc or melatonin, because the doses add up.

How far apart should I take iron and calcium?

Experts suggest taking them at different times of day, for example iron in the morning and calcium with dinner.

Can I take all my vitamins at once?

Many can be taken together, but minerals that compete, such as iron with calcium or zinc, are better spaced out. Fat-soluble vitamins such as D and K absorb best with a meal.

Which supplements interact with blood thinners?

Vitamin K can change the effect of warfarin, and fish oil can raise INR. Anyone on a blood thinner should ask their doctor before starting either.
