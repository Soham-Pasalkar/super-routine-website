Vitamin D3 + K2: dose, best time to take it and why they pair

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, FounderUpdated 21 Oct 2026

Take vitamin D3 with a meal that contains some fat, ideally at the same time each day. Breakfast works well for most people. D3 is fat-soluble and absorbs best with food. The Super Routine Day Pack contains 600 IU (15 mcg) of D3, which matches the adult daily recommendation, paired with vitamin K2.

Why vitamin D matters in India

The skin makes vitamin D from sunlight, but indoor work, pollution, clothing, sunscreen and darker skin all reduce it. Low vitamin D status is common in India despite the sunshine. Vitamin D contributes to the normal absorption of calcium, normal bones and teeth, normal muscle function and normal immune function.

Why D3 and not D2

Both forms raise blood vitamin D, but D3 (cholecalciferol) raises and maintains levels more effectively than D2 (ergocalciferol) in most studies. D3 is also the form the skin makes.

Why D3 is paired with K2

Vitamin D raises calcium absorption from the gut. Vitamin K is needed to activate proteins such as osteocalcin, which bind calcium into bone. Pairing the two is a common approach, though trials have not shown that the combination prevents fractures. We include K2 because both nutrients contribute to normal bones.

Why 600 IU and not 60,000 IU

600 IU a day is the adult Recommended Dietary Allowance (800 IU over age 70), and it is used daily to maintain levels. High-dose sachets such as 60,000 IU are given weekly by doctors to correct a diagnosed deficiency. That is treatment, not daily nutrition. The upper limit for adults is 4,000 IU a day. If you have been told you are deficient, follow your doctor's plan.

Best time to take vitamin D3

With a meal that contains some fat, at a consistent time. Breakfast is easiest to remember, which is why D3 + K2 is in the Day Pack.

Who should be careful

  • Warfarin or other vitamin K antagonists: vitamin K can reduce the effect of warfarin. Do not take a product with K2 without your doctor's approval.
  • Steroids, orlistat, some statins and thiazide diuretics can interact with vitamin D. Ask your doctor.
  • High calcium levels, kidney stones or sarcoidosis: ask your doctor first.

FAQ

What is the best time to take vitamin D3?

With a meal that contains some fat, at the same time every day. Breakfast or lunch both work.

Should vitamin D3 be taken with K2?

Many people take them together because vitamin D raises calcium absorption and vitamin K helps activate the proteins that use calcium in bone. Anyone on warfarin must speak to their doctor first.

Is 600 IU of vitamin D enough?

600 IU is the daily recommendation for adults up to 70 and is meant to maintain normal levels. People with a diagnosed deficiency usually need a doctor-prescribed higher dose for a period.
