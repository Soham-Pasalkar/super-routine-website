Omega-3 (rTG): form, dose and the best time to take it

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, Registered Pharmacist, FounderUpdated 21 Oct 2026

Omega-3 can be taken at any time of day. What matters is taking it consistently, and with a meal. The rTG (re-esterified triglyceride) form is absorbed somewhat better than the cheaper ethyl ester (EE) form. The Super Routine Night Pack contains 750 mg of rTG omega-3, taken after dinner.

What EPA and DHA do

EPA and DHA are long-chain omega-3 fats from oily fish and algae. They are built into cell membranes, especially in the brain and eyes. EPA and DHA contribute to normal heart function, and DHA contributes to normal brain function and vision. Many Indian diets, especially vegetarian ones, contain little EPA or DHA.

rTG vs EE vs natural TG

FormWhat it isAbsorption
Natural triglyceride (TG)Fish oil as it occurs in fishGood
Ethyl ester (EE)Concentrated by bonding fatty acids to ethanolSomewhat lower
Re-esterified triglyceride (rTG)Concentrated, then converted back to the triglyceride formSomewhat higher than EE

All three forms raise blood EPA and DHA. rTG gives a concentrated oil in a form closer to natural fish oil.

Best time to take omega-3: morning or night?

No study shows that one time of day is clearly better. Two things do matter:

  • Take it with a meal. Omega-3 is a fat and is handled like dietary fat, and taking it with food reduces fishy aftertaste and burps for many people.
  • Be consistent. Blood levels build over weeks of daily use, not hours.

We put omega-3 in the Night Pack so it is taken with dinner, usually the largest meal of the day in India. It also keeps the morning sachet small.

Who should be careful

  • Blood thinners such as warfarin: fish oil may slightly affect clotting. Tell your doctor, who may monitor your INR.
  • Fish or shellfish allergy: check with your doctor before taking fish oil.
  • Common mild side effects are an unpleasant taste, heartburn, nausea and loose stools.

FAQ

Should I take omega-3 in the morning or at night?

Either is fine. Take it with a meal and at the same time each day. We use dinner because it is usually the largest meal of the day.

Is rTG omega-3 better than regular fish oil?

rTG is absorbed somewhat better than ethyl ester (EE) concentrates, and is similar to natural fish oil. All forms raise EPA and DHA levels.

Can vegetarians get EPA and DHA?

Plant foods such as flax provide ALA, which the body converts to EPA and DHA only in small amounts. Algae oil is a vegetarian source of DHA and EPA. The Super Routine Night Pack uses fish oil.
