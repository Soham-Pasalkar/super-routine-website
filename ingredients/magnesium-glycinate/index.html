Magnesium glycinate: benefits, dose and when to take it

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, FounderUpdated 22 Oct 2026

Magnesium glycinate is magnesium bound to the amino acid glycine. It is gentle on the stomach and well absorbed. Most people take it in the evening. A 1000 mg dose gives about 140 mg of elemental magnesium, well within the 350 mg daily upper limit for supplements. That is the amount in the Super Routine Night Pack.

Elemental magnesium: read this before comparing labels

Labels can be confusing. "1000 mg magnesium glycinate" is the weight of the whole compound, magnesium plus glycine. Only about 14% of that weight is magnesium itself. The figure that matters is elemental magnesium: 1000 mg of glycinate gives roughly 140 mg.

The adult Recommended Dietary Allowance is 400–420 mg a day for men and 310–320 mg for women, from food and supplements combined. The upper limit for magnesium from supplements is 350 mg a day. 140 mg tops up a diet that falls short without going near that limit.

What magnesium does

Magnesium is a co-factor in more than 300 enzyme systems. It contributes to normal muscle function, normal nervous system function, energy-yielding metabolism and the reduction of tiredness and fatigue. It also helps regulate calcium movement in nerve and muscle cells.

When to take magnesium glycinate

You can take it at any time, but most people take it in the evening with dinner or before bed. It fits a wind-down routine, and evening is when the Super Routine Night Pack is taken. Evidence that magnesium improves sleep is limited and mixed, and is strongest in people whose intake is low. We do not claim it is a sleep aid.

We keep magnesium in the Night Pack and zinc in the Day Pack. Very high zinc intakes (around 142 mg a day) have been shown to reduce magnesium absorption. Our doses are far lower, but splitting the two removes any competition. See taking zinc and magnesium together.

Who should be careful

  • Antibiotics (tetracyclines such as doxycycline; quinolones such as ciprofloxacin): take the antibiotic at least 2 hours before, or 4–6 hours after, magnesium.
  • Bisphosphonates for bone health (such as alendronate): separate by at least 2 hours.
  • Diuretics and long-term proton pump inhibitors (such as omeprazole) can change magnesium levels. Ask your doctor.
  • Kidney disease: do not take magnesium supplements without medical advice.

FAQ

Is magnesium glycinate better taken in the morning or at night?

Either works, because absorption does not depend on time of day. Most people prefer the evening because it fits a wind-down routine, and that is when the Super Routine Night Pack is taken.

How much elemental magnesium is in 1000 mg of magnesium glycinate?

About 140 mg. Glycinate is roughly 14% magnesium by weight.

Can I take magnesium glycinate every day?

Yes, for most healthy adults, if total magnesium from supplements stays below 350 mg a day. People with kidney disease should ask their doctor first.

Is magnesium glycinate the same as bisglycinate?

Yes. Bisglycinate means one magnesium bound to two glycine molecules, which is what most labels mean by glycinate.
