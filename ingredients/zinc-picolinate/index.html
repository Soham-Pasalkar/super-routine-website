Zinc picolinate: dose, timing and taking it with magnesium

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, Registered Pharmacist, FounderUpdated 21 Oct 2026

Yes, most people can take zinc and magnesium together at normal doses. What zinc does

Zinc is a co-factor for hundreds of enzymes. It contributes to normal immune function, normal protein and DNA synthesis, normal skin, hair and nails, and normal fertility and reproduction. Diets heavy in cereals and legumes, which are common in India, contain phytates that reduce zinc absorption.

Why picolinate

Zinc picolinate is zinc bound to picolinic acid. A small early study found it raised zinc levels more than gluconate or citrate. The evidence is limited, but picolinate is a well-tolerated chelated form, and much better than poorly absorbed zinc oxide.

Why 12 mg

The adult RDA is 11 mg for men and 8 mg for women. 12 mg covers that need while staying far below the 40 mg upper limit. Doses of 50 mg or more a day over weeks can lower copper levels, which is why we avoid high-dose zinc.

Can I take zinc and magnesium together?

At normal supplement doses, yes. The interaction that has been measured involves very high zinc intake (around 142 mg a day), which reduced magnesium absorption. Super Routine still separates them, with zinc in the morning and magnesium glycinate at night, so the two minerals never compete.

Best time to take zinc

  • Zinc absorbs well on an empty stomach, but it can cause nausea. Taking it after breakfast, as in the Day Pack, is easier on the stomach.
  • Keep it away from iron supplements of 25 mg or more, which reduce zinc absorption.

Who should be careful

  • Antibiotics (quinolones, tetracyclines): take the antibiotic 2 hours before, or 4–6 hours after, zinc.
  • Penicillamine: separate by at least 1 hour.
  • Thiazide diuretics increase zinc loss. Ask your doctor.

FAQ

Can you take zinc and magnesium together?

Yes, at normal doses. Only very high zinc intakes (about 142 mg a day) have been shown to reduce magnesium absorption. Super Routine separates them anyway: zinc in the Day Pack, magnesium in the Night Pack.

What is the best time to take zinc?

After a meal if it upsets your stomach, and away from iron supplements. In Super Routine it is taken after breakfast.

Is zinc picolinate better than zinc gluconate?

A small study found picolinate raised zinc levels more. Evidence is limited, but both are well absorbed compared with zinc oxide.
