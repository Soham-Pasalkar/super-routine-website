Vitamin B12: methylcobalamin vs cyanocobalamin, dose and timing

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, FounderUpdated 29 Oct 2026

Both methylcobalamin and cyanocobalamin raise vitamin B12 levels. Cyanocobalamin is converted in the body, while methylcobalamin is already an active form. B12 can be taken at any time, though many people take it in the morning. The Super Routine Day Pack contains 2.5 mcg of methylcobalamin (100% RDA) as part of its 13 multivitamins.

Who is most at risk of low B12

B12 is found almost only in animal foods, so vegetarians and vegans are at higher risk, and that covers a large share of India. Absorption also falls with age, and with long-term use of metformin or acid-reducing medicines (proton pump inhibitors and H2 blockers). Low B12 can cause tiredness, poor concentration, and tingling in the hands and feet.

What B12 does

Vitamin B12 contributes to normal energy-yielding metabolism, the formation of red blood cells, normal nerve function and the reduction of tiredness and fatigue.

Honest summary: both work for most healthy people, and there is limited evidence that one is clearly superior. We chose methylcobalamin because it is already active and is widely used in Indian pharmacy B12 products.

Why 2.5 mcg, and when higher doses are used

Super Routine gives 100% of the daily requirement, not a megadose. A daily dose in the low micrograms is meant to maintain normal B12 status in people who absorb it normally, and the intrinsic factor pathway absorbs small doses like this efficiently.

High oral doses (500–1,000 mcg) or injections are used to correct a deficiency, or when absorption is poor, for example in older adults or after long-term metformin or acid-reducing medicines. At those doses only about 1% is absorbed, by passive diffusion. That is treatment and should be guided by your doctor. No upper limit has been set for B12, because excess is excreted.

Best time to take vitamin B12

B12 can be taken at any time, with or without food. We put it in the Day Pack because it supports energy metabolism and fits the morning routine with the other multivitamins, including vitamin D3, and the trace minerals.

FAQ

Is methylcobalamin better than cyanocobalamin?

Both raise B12 levels. Methylcobalamin is an active form and cyanocobalamin is converted in the body. Evidence that one is clearly better for healthy people is limited.

What is the best time to take vitamin B12?

Any time, with or without food. Many people take it in the morning, which is when it is in the Super Routine Day Pack.

Can I take B12 if I am on metformin?

Metformin can lower B12 levels over time, so B12 is often recommended alongside it. Ask your doctor to check your levels.
