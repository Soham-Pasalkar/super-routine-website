L-theanine + Panax ginseng: dose, research and safety

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, FounderUpdated 29 Oct 2026

L-theanine is an amino acid from tea leaves studied for calm focus; Panax ginseng is studied for mental performance and fatigue. Neither contains caffeine. L-theanine: what the research says

L-theanine is an amino acid found naturally in tea. In a 4-week randomised trial in healthy adults, 200 mg a day improved stress-related symptoms and some measures of attention and sleep compared with placebo. Other small studies used 100–400 mg. The evidence is promising but limited, so we describe L-theanine as studied for calm focus, not as a treatment.

Panax ginseng: what the research says

Panax ginseng (Asian or Korean ginseng) is one of the most studied herbal ingredients. Trials have looked at mental performance, fatigue and wellbeing, mostly with 200–400 mg a day of standardised extract, with mixed but encouraging results. The NCCIH notes that the evidence is not conclusive. Standardising the extract to its ginsenoside content means each dose is consistent.

Why they are paired in the morning

Both fit the start of the day, when focus and energy matter, and neither is a stimulant. They sit in the Day Pack with the 13 multivitamins and 7 trace minerals.

Who should be careful

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: avoid Panax ginseng.
  • Diabetes medicine: ginseng may lower blood sugar. Monitor your levels and tell your doctor.
  • Warfarin and other blood thinners: ginseng may change their effect. Ask your doctor.
  • Antidepressants (MAOIs), stimulants or blood pressure medicines: ask your doctor before use.
  • Ginseng can cause sleep problems in some people, which is one reason it is taken in the morning.

FAQ

Does L-theanine make you sleepy?

Not at 200 mg in most people. It is studied for calm focus and relaxation without drowsiness, which is why it is in the morning sachet.

Does Panax ginseng contain caffeine?

No. Ginseng is a root and contains no caffeine.

How much L-theanine and ginseng is in Super Routine?

200 mg of L-theanine and 200 mg of Panax ginseng root extract standardised to 3% ginsenosides, in the Day Pack.

Who should avoid Panax ginseng?

Pregnant or breastfeeding women, and anyone on diabetes medicine, blood thinners or MAOI antidepressants unless their doctor agrees.
