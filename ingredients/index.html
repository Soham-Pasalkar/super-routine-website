Super Routine ingredients: 25 nutrients in two sachets, with forms, doses and timing

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, FounderUpdated 29 Oct 2026

Super Routine has 25 nutrients in two sachets. The Day Pack, taken after breakfast, holds 13 vitamins and 7 trace minerals at 100% RDA plus L-theanine and Panax ginseng. The Night Pack, taken after dinner, holds magnesium glycinate, ashwagandha KSM-66 and omega-3 fish oil. Each was chosen for a well-absorbed form and a dose within the range used in published studies or set by Indian requirements.

How our doses are set

Vitamins and minerals are dosed at 100% of the RDA, to fill everyday gaps without megadoses. Herbal ingredients and other nutrients are dosed within the ranges used in human studies and permitted by FSSAI. We choose forms that absorb well, such as chelated minerals, methylcobalamin, L-methylfolate and rTG omega-3. Every dose stays below the tolerable upper intake level where one exists. Every amount is printed on the pack, with no proprietary blends.

FAQ

Which supplements should not be taken together?

Some combinations compete or interact. High-dose zinc and magnesium compete for absorption, iron reduces zinc absorption, vitamin K affects warfarin, and minerals bind some antibiotics. That is why Super Routine splits the trace minerals (Day) and magnesium (Night) and lists every interaction on each ingredient page.

Why a day and night split instead of one multivitamin?

Timing matters for some nutrients. Fat-soluble vitamins absorb best with a meal, ashwagandha fits an evening wind-down, and keeping zinc and magnesium apart avoids competition between them.

How many nutrients are in Super Routine?

25: 13 vitamins, 7 trace minerals, L-theanine and Panax ginseng in the Day Pack, and magnesium, ashwagandha and omega-3 in the Night Pack.
