How our doses are set

We start from the daily intake levels set by health authorities, and from the doses used in human studies. We choose forms that absorb well, such as chelated minerals, methylcobalamin and rTG omega-3. Every dose stays below the tolerable upper intake level where one exists. Every amount is printed on the pack, with no proprietary blends.

FAQ

Which supplements should not be taken together?

Some combinations compete or interact. High-dose zinc and magnesium compete for absorption, iron reduces zinc absorption, vitamin K affects warfarin, and minerals bind some antibiotics. That is why Super Routine splits zinc (Day) and magnesium (Night) and lists every interaction on each ingredient page.

Why a day and night split instead of one multivitamin?

Timing matters for some nutrients. Melatonin belongs at bedtime, fat-soluble vitamins absorb best with a meal, and keeping zinc and magnesium apart avoids competition between them.
