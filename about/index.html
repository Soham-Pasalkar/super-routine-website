About Super Routine supplements

Super Routine is a day and night supplement system being built in India by founder Shubham Pasalkar. 25 essential nutrients, two sachets, every dose on the pack, and no proprietary blends.

The founder

Shubham Pasalkar started Super Routine to solve a simple problem: people trying to look after their health were buying a shelf of separate jars, guessing at doses and timing, and paying around ₹3,000 a month to do it. Super Routine puts 25 essential nutrients into one Day sachet and one Night sachet, at clear, studied doses.

Shubham writes and reviews every ingredient page on this site.

Our standards

  • Clean: only what is necessary, with no fillers or extras.
  • Transparent: every ingredient, form and amount is on the pack. No proprietary blends.
  • Evidence-led: doses and forms come from published human studies and from intake levels set by health authorities.
  • Honest: we say what each nutrient does and what it does not. We do not claim to treat, cure or prevent any disease.

How we write and review content

Every ingredient page is based on primary sources such as the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements and peer-reviewed studies, which are linked at the bottom of each page. Each page states the dose, form and timing, who should not take the ingredient, and its known interactions with medicines. Pages are reviewed whenever the science or our formula changes.

Testing

Before the first drop, each batch will be tested by an independent laboratory for identity, content and contaminants, including heavy metals. Results will be published for founding members.

Where we are now

We are building in public. The research and formulation are in progress, and testing and the Founding Drop come next. Join the founding list to follow along and vote on what we make next.

Not medical advice. Super Routine is a food supplement, not a medicine, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. Speak to your doctor before use if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, under 18, have a medical condition or take any medicine.
