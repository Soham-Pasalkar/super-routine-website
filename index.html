01 / The Super Routine

Two Packs. One for Day. One for Night.

Super Routine splits eight essentials into a Day Pack and a Night Pack. Take one sachet in the morning and one before bed. A 30-day supply is 30 day sachets and 30 night sachets. Tap any supplement for the full details.

Day Pack

Energy · Focus · Immunity · Bone

Dosage + Form
600 IU (15 mcg) matches the daily recommended intake for most adults, so it maintains levels well below the 4,000 IU upper limit. D3 raises blood levels more effectively than D2, and K2 is paired with it so calcium goes where it is needed.
Mechanism of Action
The liver and kidneys convert D3 into calcitriol, the active hormone that raises calcium absorption in the gut. K2 activates osteocalcin, the protein that binds calcium into bone.
Health Benefits
Supports bones and teeth, muscle function and normal immune function.
Problem It Solves
Low vitamin D from limited sun exposure, which is common in India, and the bone aches and muscle weakness that follow.
Dosage + Form
1 mg (1,000 mcg) is a standard supplement dose. Only a small share is absorbed at high doses, so it reliably rebuilds low stores, even with weaker absorption. Methylcobalamin is the active form, ready to use without conversion.
Mechanism of Action
Works as a co-factor for enzymes that make red blood cells, build the nerve-protecting myelin sheath and turn homocysteine into methionine.
Health Benefits
Supports energy metabolism, red blood cell formation and normal nerve function.
Problem It Solves
B12 shortfall in vegetarians, vegans and people on metformin or acid reducers, causing tiredness, tingling and poor focus.
Dosage + Form
12 mg is close to the daily adult requirement and well under the 40 mg upper limit. Picolinate is a chelated form that absorbs better than zinc oxide.
Mechanism of Action
A co-factor for 300+ enzymes, supporting DNA and protein synthesis, immune cell development and wound repair.
Health Benefits
Supports immunity, skin and wound healing, and normal testosterone and fertility function.
Problem It Solves
Low zinc from cereal- and legume-heavy diets, which can mean frequent infections, slow healing and low appetite.
Dosage + Form
250 mg is within the range used in human studies (250 to 500 mg a day). Purified resin is used because raw shilajit can carry heavy metals.
Mechanism of Action
Fulvic acid and dibenzo-alpha-pyrones may support mitochondrial energy production, act as antioxidants and help carry minerals into cells.
Health Benefits
May support energy, stamina and healthy testosterone levels in men. Evidence is early.
Problem It Solves
Low energy and stamina from daily stress and fatigue, with a long history of use in Ayurveda.

Night Pack

Recovery · Sleep · Muscle · Wellness

Dosage + Form
1000 mg of glycinate gives 140 mg of elemental magnesium, about a third of the daily need. It tops up the diet without the laxative effect of higher doses or magnesium oxide. Glycinate pairs magnesium with glycine, which is calming and absorbs well.
Mechanism of Action
Regulates calcium flow in muscles and nerves, activates calming GABA receptors in the brain and acts as a co-factor in ATP energy production.
Health Benefits
Supports muscle relaxation and recovery, a calmer mind and better sleep quality.
Problem It Solves
Night-time cramps, muscle tension and restless sleep from low magnesium intake or stress.
Dosage + Form
300 mg sits in the range used in trials (300 to 600 mg a day). KSM-66 is a full-spectrum, root-only extract standardised to withanolides, unlike generic powders that may include leaf.
Mechanism of Action
Withanolides help moderate the cortisol stress response and influence GABA signalling, which helps the body wind down.
Health Benefits
Supports stress resilience, sleep quality, recovery and strength gains with training.
Problem It Solves
Chronic stress and high evening cortisol that keep the mind switched on and sleep light.
Dosage + Form
750 mg of omega-3 is a meaningful daily step toward the 250 to 500 mg of EPA + DHA that health bodies suggest. rTG is a concentrated form that absorbs better than ethyl ester fish oil.
Mechanism of Action
EPA and DHA are built into cell membranes, where they calm inflammatory signalling and keep membranes flexible, especially in the brain, eyes and heart.
Health Benefits
Supports heart health, healthy triglyceride levels, brain function and joint comfort.
Problem It Solves
Low omega-3 intake in diets with little oily fish, linked to high triglycerides and stiffness.
Dosage + Form
3 mg is a widely used dose that works for most adults. Higher doses add grogginess, not benefit.
Mechanism of Action
Acts on MT1 and MT2 receptors in the brain's body clock to signal that it is night and lower alertness.
Health Benefits
Helps you fall asleep faster and resets the body clock after late nights, shift work or travel.
Problem It Solves
Delayed sleep from irregular schedules, late-night screen light and jet lag.
