Super Routine Night Pack: your evening supplements in one sachet

Written and reviewed by Shubham Pasalkar, FounderUpdated 29 Oct 2026

The Night Pack holds three nutrients that suit the evening: magnesium glycinate (2000 mg, giving 280 mg of elemental magnesium), ashwagandha KSM-66 (600 mg root-only extract) and omega-3 deep sea fish oil (1000 mg of EPA + DHA, rTG form). Take one sachet after dinner.

Why these three go at night

Magnesium and ashwagandha fit a wind-down routine. Ashwagandha's sleep and stress trials are a key reason it is in the formula, and most people prefer magnesium in the evening. See magnesium glycinate and ashwagandha together.

Omega-3 is taken with dinner, usually the largest meal of the day in India. Fish oil is absorbed like dietary fat and causes fewer fishy burps when taken with food.

Magnesium is kept apart from the Day Pack's zinc, so the two minerals never compete for absorption.

The Night Pack contains no melatonin and no sedatives. It supports the evening routine rather than forcing sleep.

How to take it

  • Take one Night sachet after dinner with water.
  • Take one Day sachet after breakfast.
  • Do not take more than one Night sachet a day.
  • If you take blood thinners, thyroid medicine, sedatives, blood pressure or diabetes medicine, or antibiotics, ask your doctor or pharmacist before use. Avoid it if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.

FAQ

Does the Night Pack contain melatonin?

No. The Night Pack contains magnesium glycinate, ashwagandha KSM-66 and omega-3 fish oil. There is no melatonin and no sedative.

When should I take the Night Pack?

After dinner, at about the same time each evening. Omega-3 absorbs best with a meal.

How much elemental magnesium is in the Night Pack?

280 mg, from 2000 mg of magnesium glycinate. That is below the 350 mg a day upper limit for magnesium from supplements.

Is the Night Pack vegetarian?

No. The omega-3 comes from deep sea fish oil.
